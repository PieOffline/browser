# Browser

A modern browser project with integrated AI capabilities.

## Copilot Mode

**Copilot Mode** is an experimental browsing experience designed to blend AI assistance directly into the browser. It creates a unified homepage where search, chat, and navigation work together, keeping Copilot visible as you browse.

### Key AI Capabilities

#### Hands-Free Browsing
Use voice commands to navigate pages and highlight content without touching your keyboard or mouse.

#### Smarter Tab and History Understanding
Copilot can summarize open tabs and past browsing to give more relevant answers based on your context.

#### Instant Help In-Tab
Ask questions without leaving the page you're on - get contextual assistance right where you need it.

#### Early-Access AI Features
- **Copilot Journeys**: Remembers your browsing path to help you pick up where you left off
- **Copilot Actions**: Automates small repetitive tasks to save you time

### Additional AI Tools

| Feature | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| Video Summarization | Get quick summaries of video content |
| Image Creation | Generate images using AI directly in the browser |
| Real-Time Video Translation | Translate video content in real-time |
| AI-Powered Tab Organization | Automatically organize and group your tabs intelligently |

### Privacy Controls

Copilot Mode is designed with privacy in mind:
- All AI features are optional and can be disabled
- Switch Copilot Mode on or off at any time
- Your data is handled according to our privacy policy

## Getting Started

Copilot Mode is currently in experimental preview. To enable it, look for the Copilot Mode toggle in your browser settings.
